DIAGRAM DESCRIPTIONS FOR HLA-PROTBERT PRESENTATION

1) transformer_diagram.pdf/png
   - Visualizes the encoder stack shared across the supported models (ProtBERT, ESM-2,
     ProtT5, Ankh). Shows token embeddings, positional handling, multi-head attention,
     feed-forward blocks, layer norm, and pooled output used as the HLA embedding.

2) dim_reduction_diagram.pdf/png
   - Compares PCA (global variance), t-SNE (local clusters), and UMAP (balanced local/global)
     as used in `scripts/analyze_locus_embeddings.py`. Highlights that PCA gives a quick global
     view while t-SNE/UMAP surface allele-family structure.

3) Locus plots (sourced from the pipeline output)
   - Files under `data/analysis/locus_embeddings/class1/plots/*` (e.g., `hla_A_umap.png`).
   - Generated by the ProtBERT embeddings in the default pipeline run; can be regenerated by
     running `./run_complete_pipeline_all_encoders.sh` then `scripts/run_locus_analysis.py`.
